Stage 7C acceptance
===================

- every Stage 7A accepted product has a positive EGP retail price
- retail-price provenance stays PUBLIC_MARKET_EGYPT
- every purchase cost is positive, <= retail price, and SYNTHETIC_CALIBRATED
- modeled gross margin stays inside declared simulation ranges

- sample transaction lines reconcile gross -> discount -> net -> COGS -> gross profit
